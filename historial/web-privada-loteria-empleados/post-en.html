A private website for employees' Christmas Lottery makes it possible to organise access to the campaign from a space set up for the purpose. At Lotería Las Arenas we can create that password-protected virtual space, along with the instructions that correspond to the options agreed.

For the organiser, the point is not simply having a link. What matters is that the team knows which number they can buy, until when, how to take part and what happens to the décimos (lottery tickets) after purchase. If you wish, you can start with our online company lottery service.

The idea

A point of reference, not a magic solution

The private space is a point of reference for the team, not a magic solution. Before distributing access, it is worth settling the number, the approximate amount and the options, and confirming the provisional hold. A well-prepared space helps you communicate clearly, but it does not by itself prevent posters from previous years, wrong dates or confused options from circulating.

First the campaign is defined; then access is shared

 

Before distributing the space, we settle the number preference, the approximate amount and how the purchases will be organised. We check availability and confirm the relevant provisional hold.

We also agree whether deposit, individual delivery, group delivery or other options envisaged for your campaign will be used. A company with its entire staff at a single site may need a different arrangement from one whose employees work remotely.

When the platform is prepared, we provide the password, the poster and the instructions as agreed. It is not advisable to announce options or dates before that information has been defined.

What it offers the company's organiser

 

The space offers a point of reference for directing employees, instead of handling every query with a separate message. The company can communicate access and the terms consistently across its internal channels.

If the option allows each employee to buy online, the organiser does not have to personally collect the amount for those purchases. That is a concrete difference from managing a common pool; even so, it must not be confused with promising that all coordination work disappears.

Its usefulness depends on clear communication. A properly prepared space does not by itself prevent posters from previous years from circulating, a wrong date from being announced or the options from being confused.

What HR should communicate to the team

 

The launch communication should identify the draw and the year, the number or numbers proposed, the access provided and the options enabled. Include your campaign's deadline and a contact for questions.

→ The draw and the year, and the number or numbers proposed.

→ The deadline for your campaign and a contact for questions.

→ If there is group delivery, how the internal distribution will be communicated.

→ If there is online deposit, that the décimos are kept in safekeeping and that this purchase is not made in order to collect them later.

Distribute the password through the designated internal channel. The existence of private access does not authorise sharing buyers' information or passing on personal user credentials.

The chosen option determines the subsequent work

 

With online purchase on deposit there is no need to organise a physical distribution of those décimos. In a campaign with group delivery, by contrast, someone must receive them and coordinate their distribution against the list of buyers. Home deliveries require the conditions and permitted destinations for each order, and in-person purchase or direct sale on the premises are other routes that must be agreed and communicated separately.

The best setup is not the one with the most options, but the one that employees understand and the company can coordinate.

An organisational example. Imagine a company with staff who come into the office and staff who work remotely. Before launching, the organiser asks which options can be offered to both groups; once these are agreed, they send a single communication with the access and set out the instructions for each option separately, and they identify the person responsible for receiving deliveries if there is group delivery. This is an example of how to organise it, not a setup that every company has enabled automatically: the space should reflect your specific arrangement.

What is worth asking before you start

 

Ask which options can be enabled, what information we need to receive in order to set them up and when the campaign can be communicated. Also ask how group delivery will be coordinated, if you need it.

Do not assume integrations with payroll, corporate access, reporting dashboards or automatic features that have not been agreed. The service should be described in terms of what is actually available for your company.
