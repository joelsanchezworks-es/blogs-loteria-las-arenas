Buying your company's Christmas Lottery online: how it works

To buy your company's Christmas Lottery online, use the access and instructions the organiser has provided. Check the number, the draw, the deadline and, above all, the option you're buying: leaving your décimos on deposit is not the same as requesting physical delivery.

At Lotería Las Arenas we can set up private, password-protected virtual spaces for company campaigns. The terms are agreed for each organisation, so you must follow those of your own campaign, not those of another company or an old guide. If you're the person in charge, you can read about our Christmas Lottery for companies service.

The key point

Online deposit and physical delivery are not the same thing

Before you pay, be clear about what you're buying. With an online deposit, your décimos are held in safekeeping at the lottery office: this is not a purchase you then go and collect later, nor are they sent out on their own at a later date. If you need the décimo in your hand, choose a physical delivery option (home delivery or group delivery), where your campaign has it enabled. And always follow your company's instructions, not those from another year or another platform.

Before you log in, find the right instructions

 

Check the message your company has sent you. It should let you identify the campaign, the access provided and the purchase options. If you don't have it, or you're not sure it's for this year, check before continuing.

Also check that you're on the website specified by the organisation. The Lotería Las Arenas space and a campaign that uses another platform are separate services; their access details and terms should not be mixed.

And look after your data: don't post company passwords on open networks. If there's a problem with the access, ask the campaign contact for help rather than giving your personal passwords or payment details to third parties.

Check the number and the draw

 

A number matching the one you remember isn't enough to identify your purchase. Check that it corresponds to the draw and the year of the campaign, and that you're choosing the quantity you actually want to buy.

If your company offers several numbers, decide which ones you want to buy within your budget. Taking part is voluntary: you don't have to buy them all to be part of the campaign.

The availability you were told about when the campaign opened may not match what you find when you come to buy. If you don't see what you expected, check before assuming there's an individual reservation in your name.

Check the details before you confirm

 

Before you pay, check the number, draw, quantity, option and total amount. If delivery is involved, also check the related information. Use only the payment methods that are enabled and the guidance shown in the actual process.

This guide doesn't replace the notices and terms that appear when you make your purchase. The names of buttons or screens may change; the key thing is to confirm what you're buying and which option.

If an error comes up, don't automatically assume the purchase has gone through, and don't repeat the payment without checking the situation. Check the transaction details and ask for help if you're still in doubt.

Keep the details of your purchase

 

Keep the receipt or confirmation you're given. It's a useful reference for looking into a problem or clarifying which quantity and option you chose.

Watch out for two common mix-ups: replying to the internal email with an 'I want one' does not replace the online purchase, and keeping the access instructions does not prove you've bought a décimo.

For group deliveries, follow the organiser's instructions. If you chose safekeeping, don't expect a physical handout that doesn't match your option. And for anything to do with prizes, check the procedure that applies to that purchase: don't assume it's the same as another platform's.

Frequently asked questions about buying your company's lottery online

 
What's the difference between online deposit and physical delivery?

With an online deposit, your décimos are held in safekeeping at the lottery office: this is not a purchase you then go and collect later, nor are they sent out automatically at a later date. With physical delivery (home delivery or group delivery at the company), you do receive the décimo, subject to the terms of your campaign. They are different options, so choose the one you really need and don't use the deposit as a shortcut for a collection that isn't provided for.

Do I have to buy all the numbers my company offers?

No. Taking part is voluntary, and you don't have to buy all the numbers to be part of the campaign. If your company offers several, decide which ones you want to buy within your budget. Only buy the quantity you actually want.

How do I know I'm buying on the right website?

Always use the access provided by your organisation and check that it's the website specified for your campaign. The Lotería Las Arenas space and a campaign that uses another platform are separate services, with access details and terms that should not be mixed. If you're unsure, check with the campaign contact before continuing, and never post company passwords on open networks.

What should I check before confirming payment?

Check the number, the draw, the quantity, the option and the total amount (including delivery, if there is any). Use only the enabled payment methods and the guidance on the actual screen. The names of buttons or screens may change; what matters is to confirm what you're buying and which option before you pay.

If an error appears when paying, should I repeat the purchase?

Don't repeat it without checking. If an error appears, don't automatically assume the purchase has gone through, and don't pay again without checking the situation. Check the transaction details and, if there's still any doubt, ask the campaign contact for help before trying again.

How do I check whether my décimo has won a prize?

It depends on your purchase option: for anything to do with prizes, check the procedure that applies to that purchase and don't assume it's the same as another platform's. Always keep the receipt or confirmation you're given; it's the reference for clarifying what you bought and sorting out any problem. If you chose safekeeping, don't expect a physical handout that doesn't match that option.
