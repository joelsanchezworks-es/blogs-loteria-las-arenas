At an official lottery office, buying online doesn't make the décimo more expensive: you pay the same price as at the counter. The décimo costs €20, whether you buy it online or in person. There's no 'convenience surcharge'.

Watch out for resale websites

 

Here's the real risk: websites that sell décimos well above the official price. It's not a commission; it's resale, and it can cost you quite a bit more for the same décimo. Every year we see people who, without knowing it, have overpaid by buying where they shouldn't have.

The rule is simple: always buy from the website of an official lottery office, with its number visible (ours is 336). If the price shoots up compared to the official €20, be wary.

How to spot a markup in two seconds

Look at the price per décimo: if it's much higher than €20, something doesn't add up. Shipping may add some reasonable costs, but not multiply the price. When in doubt, buy from an official lottery office and you'll pay what it's worth, not a euro more.
