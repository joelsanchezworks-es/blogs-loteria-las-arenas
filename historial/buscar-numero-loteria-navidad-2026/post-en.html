Frequently asked questions about finding your Christmas Lottery number

 
How do I search for a specific Christmas Lottery number?

With an online décimo finder: you go in, type the full number or just the ending (the last two digits) and see what's available. You can also search by a date (a birthday, an anniversary). If your exact number doesn't come up, the finder itself helps you find close alternatives, all without leaving the house.

Which numbers sell out soonest?

The ones most people look for: special dates (birthdays, anniversaries, the coming year), popular endings, palindrome numbers and numbers that are easy to remember. So if your number has a special meaning, it's worth searching for it and reserving it as soon as possible: by December many have already sold out.

What do I do if my number is no longer available?

You don't miss out on playing. You can look for the same number in another series, a very close date, the same ending on another number, or join a share of that number if someone's playing it as a group. These are alternatives that keep the excitement alive. And remember that all numbers have the same probability: the choice is sentimental, not strategic.

Is a 'pretty' number or a date more likely to win?

No. In the Christmas Lottery all numbers have exactly the same probability of winning a prize, whatever shape they take. Choosing a birthday, a palindrome number or an ending is a matter of excitement, not of increasing your chances of winning. The finder just helps you find the number you like best.

Can I reserve the number online and receive a physical décimo?

Yes. Reserving or buying online at an official lottery office doesn't mean ending up with a 'virtual' number: your décimo is official and physical. You can leave it in deposit and safekeeping at the lottery office (they look after it and check it) or request home delivery, depending on the options available. Either way, the décimo is yours and is kept safe until the draw.

When's the best time to buy the Christmas Lottery?

As early as possible, especially if you're after a number with a special meaning. The most sought-after numbers (dates, palindrome numbers, popular endings) sell out weeks in advance, so waiting until December greatly reduces your chances of finding exactly the one you want. Searching early also lets you calmly choose an alternative if the exact one is no longer around.
