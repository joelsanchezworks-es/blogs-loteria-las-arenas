Our version of the ad we all wait for

 

The Christmas Lottery ad is almost a tradition in its own right. It's run by Loterías y Apuestas del Estado (Spain's state lottery operator), it airs on television and, every year, it leaves us all with a lump in our throat. It's part of the holidays.

We're not the TV: we're your usual lottery shop. But this year we also wanted to tell our side of it, in our own way of seeing Christmas. It lasts just a few seconds, we created it ourselves, and above all it's about you and your people.

There are numbers that come back every year

 

Almost all of us have a number. The birthday one, the house number, the one that won a prize once and we never let go of again. We play it year after year and, come December, we look for it the way you reach for an old habit.

That little ritual — playing the same number again — is half of Christmas. It isn't superstition: it's belonging. It's saying 'this one's ours' and feeling that, somehow, the year is starting over.

And people to share them with

 

A décimo almost never belongs to just one person. It belongs to the family, the gang, the workmates, the neighbours you've known forever. Everyone chips in their share, and with it a 'maybe this is our year…'.

Sharing a number means sharing the excitement, but also the memories: who kept it safe, who made the photocopies, who said every year they were quitting and never did. The same old faces.

You lose them along the way… and you find them again

 

And then there's the part the ads don't usually show: people change. Some move away, others we miss around the table, with some we fall out and make up again. Life, just happening.

That's why a shared number is also a way of staying close. Of calling in December — 'hey, shall we play again?' — and winning back, if only over a décimo, the same old faces. Sometimes the lottery is the perfect excuse to find each other again.

Frequently asked questions about our ad and your Christmas décimo

 
Where can I watch your Christmas ad?

It's on our Instagram: you can watch the ad here. It's a few seconds about the usual things — a décimo, your people and the longing for the draw to arrive. If you like it, share it with whoever you play with every year.

Is it the official Christmas Lottery ad?

No. The official ad you see on television is the one from Loterías y Apuestas del Estado. This is ours, the Lotería Las Arenas one: our very own version, created by us (with a little help from artificial intelligence), to wish you a Merry Christmas our way.

Can I play the same number every year?

Plenty of people do: they play their same old number out of habit or affection. If you want to secure yours for this Christmas, the safest thing is to ask for it early, because a specific number can sell out. Drop by the lottery office or write to us and we'll help you.

How do I share a décimo with my family or friends?

You can play a décimo between several people or split it into shares (participaciones), so everyone puts in their part. A tip: put in writing who's in and for how much, and keep a photo or copy of the décimo. That way the excitement is shared without any mix-ups.
