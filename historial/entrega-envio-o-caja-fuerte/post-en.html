Home delivery or the safe: what do you do with your décimo bought online?

You buy your Christmas Lottery décimo (a tenth-share lottery ticket) online and, right away, it's decision time: do we send it to your home or keep it for you? We explain both options so you can choose yours. (And then tell us: A or B?)

🎨

Banner · illustration of two delivery options for the décimo: home delivery or safe

When you buy your décimo online, you choose how to receive it. There are two paths: Option A, we send it to your home; Option B, it stays kept in our safe, in your name. Both are good and safe; the difference is whether you want the paper in hand or prefer not to have to keep it safe yourself.

We'll walk you through each one in detail and, at the end, you tell us which team you're on.

But first, if you don't have it yet, you can order your Christmas Lottery décimo online and choose the delivery when you pay.

In brief

A or B, you choose

Option A — Home delivery: we send it to you in 24-48 h across Spain (the islands too) and you have the paper in hand. Option B — Safe: we keep it in your name, free of charge, we send you the photo and, if it wins, we let you know. Both play exactly the same; only who keeps the décimo changes.

Option A: home delivery in 24-48 h

 

If you like having the physical décimo, this one's for you. We deliver it to your home, usually within 24-48 h, across Spain, the islands too. You have the paper in hand, which for many people is part of the excitement (and ideal if it's a gift).

Keep in mind that home delivery may carry some shipping costs depending on the area; you'll see it clearly when you place the order.

Option B: in our safe, free of charge

 

If you'd rather not keep track of the paper, we'll keep it for you. The décimo stays in our safe in your name, free of charge. We send you the photo so you have it identified and, if it wins, we let you know.

It's the most convenient option and the safest against the most typical mistake: buying the décimo early and losing it along the way. Here that doesn't happen.

A or B? How to choose

 

There's no better option: it depends on what you value most.

✓ Option A if you want the paper in hand or it's a gift you'll hand over in person.

✓ Option B if you prioritize the convenience and peace of mind of not having to keep it yourself (and free of charge).

✓ Both play in the same draw, with the same odds and at the official price.

If you're interested in the delivery details, we go into more depth in our guide to home delivery of lottery.

In both cases, your décimo is in your name

Whether you choose A or B, your décimo is registered in your name when you buy it at an official lottery office. We are Administración Oficial nº 336. That gives you backing whether you keep it at home or we keep it for you: the prize goes to its rightful owner, no fuss.

Frequently asked questions about delivery of the décimo bought online

 
How long does home delivery take?

Usually 24-48 h, across Spain, the islands too. When you place the order you'll see the timeframe and, if applicable, the shipping costs for your area.

Does keeping the décimo in the safe cost anything?

No. Safekeeping is free: we keep the décimo in your name, we send you the photo and, if it wins, we let you know. It's the most convenient way not to lose it.

If you keep it, how do I know it's mine?

Because it's registered in your name and we send you the photo of your décimo by email. We are Administración Oficial nº 336: the décimo and its prize are yours, whether you keep it or we keep it.

Can I change my option later?

It's best to decide when you buy, but if a question comes up, write to us or drop by the lottery office and we'll look at your case. We're in the C.C. Arenas de Barcelona.

Does a shipped décimo play the same as one in safekeeping?

Exactly the same. Same draw, same odds, same price. The delivery only changes who keeps the paper until 22 December.

And if it's a gift?

Then Option A (home delivery) suits you, to hand over the physical décimo in person. If you just want to play it yourself, Option B (the safe) is the most convenient.
