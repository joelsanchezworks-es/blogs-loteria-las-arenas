Frequently asked questions about buying a Christmas superdécimo online

 
How do you buy a Christmas superdécimo online?

In four steps: go to the lottery office's official website and head to the superdécimo section, choose your number from those available, identify yourself or create your account and pay by card at the official price. Afterwards you receive the confirmation, with the superdécimo in your name, and you choose home delivery or safekeeping (custody).

Is it safe to buy a superdécimo online?

Yes, as long as you do it on the official website of a certified lottery office. In that case you have the same guarantees as over the counter: official superdécimo, official price and a purchase registered in your name. The risk is not 'online' in itself, but buying from unauthorised websites or at an inflated price.

Does the superdécimo cost more if you buy it online?

No. You pay the official price of the décimo that the superdécimo includes, whether you buy it online or over the counter, with no commissions or surcharges for doing it online. If a website charges you extra, it is a warning sign: be wary of overpricing.

What do I receive after paying?

You receive a confirmation or receipt of the purchase, with your superdécimo registered in your name. From there, you decide whether you want it sent to your home or to leave it in safekeeping (custody) at the lottery office, where it is stored and checked until the draw.

Can I leave the superdécimo in safekeeping (custody)?

Yes. Safekeeping (custody) is a very convenient option: the lottery office stores your superdécimo and checks it for you, with no risk of you losing it or it getting damaged. It is especially handy if you buy it in advance or if it is for a syndicate. You can also request home delivery if you prefer to have it in hand.

Does the superdécimo have better chances of winning a prize?

No. The superdécimo is an official décimo in a special edition: it has the same probability and the same prize as any other décimo. What makes it special is the presentation, designed to give as a gift or to collect, not the chances of winning.
