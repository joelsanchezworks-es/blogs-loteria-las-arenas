Every year it's the same story: in December everyone rushes for their Christmas Lottery, and many discover that their number is no longer available. Buying it in October takes that problem off your plate and lets you enjoy the anticipation in good time.

Here are the compelling reasons not to leave it to the last minute.

In brief

Four good reasons to buy it now

In October you'll find more numbers available, you choose at your leisure, you avoid the rush of December deliveries and you spread the cost more evenly. The numbers you want sell out sooner

 

Numbers with meaning — birthdays, special dates, popular endings, palindrome numbers — are the first to fly off the shelves. By December, many are already sold out, and you're left making do with whatever's left.

Buying in October gives you far more room to find exactly the one you want (or a close alternative you also like) instead of searching against the clock when half the country is already buying at once.

You choose at your leisure, not in a rush

 

With time on your side, buying is a pleasure, not a race. You can browse numbers at your leisure, decide how many décimos you want, organise the office or family syndicate and sort out any questions without the stress.

In December, by contrast, everything is done in a hurry: queues, overloaded websites and rushed decisions. Buying in October is, quite simply, more convenient.

You avoid the rush (and the scares) with deliveries

 

If you buy online and want your décimo delivered to your home, time is on your side. In October, a delivery arrives with no stress; in December, with the peak in purchases and Christmas parcels, any leeway narrows.

Buying in advance lets you receive your décimo with peace of mind or, if you prefer, leave it in deposit and safekeeping at the lottery office, checked and stored until the draw. No last-minute dashes.

You spread out the cost of Christmas

 

December is already packed: presents, dinners, trips… If you leave the lottery until then as well, all the spending bunches into the same few weeks.

Buying your décimo in October lets you plan it within your budget and tick it off the list in good time, spreading out your Christmas spending more evenly. Always play responsibly and only what you planned to spend.

Frequently asked questions about buying the Christmas Lottery in October

 
Is it really better to buy the Christmas Lottery in October?

If you want a specific number, yes: in October there's far more availability. Numbers with meaning (dates, popular endings, palindrome numbers) sell out weeks in advance, so buying early gives you more chances of finding exactly the one you want. On top of that, you choose at your leisure, you avoid the rush of December deliveries and you spread the cost more evenly.

Can I buy now and receive the décimo later?

Yes. You can buy online in October and choose how you want it: have it delivered to your home with plenty of time to spare, or leave it in deposit and safekeeping at the lottery office, where it's checked and stored until the draw. Buying in advance is precisely what lets you avoid the rush of December deliveries.

Does the price change if I buy in October?

No. At an official lottery office you always pay the official price for the décimo (20 €), whether you buy it in October or December, with no commissions or surcharges. What you gain by buying early isn't price, but availability, peace of mind and time to get organised.

Is it safe to buy the Christmas Lottery online so far in advance?

Yes, as long as you do so at a certified official lottery office of Lotería Nacional. Your décimo is official and physical, and you can leave it safeguarded and checked until the draw. Buying early adds no risk at all: on the contrary, it secures the number for you before it sells out.

What if I want to organise a syndicate with my family or my office?

All the more reason to start early. In October you have time to agree on the number, how many décimos and how you split the stake, without the December rush. And if you play as a group, put in writing who's taking part and with how much, so the split is clean if you win.

Until when can I buy the Christmas Lottery?

You can buy it right up to the day of the draw (the 22 December), but waiting that long has a cost: the most sought-after numbers will be gone. That's why we recommend not leaving it to the last minute. Buying in October secures the number you want and lets you enjoy the anticipation in good time.
