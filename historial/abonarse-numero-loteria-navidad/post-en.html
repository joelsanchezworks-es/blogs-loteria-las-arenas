What does subscribing to a number mean?

 

To subscribe is to reserve a specific number to play every year, so you don't depend on whether it's still available when you go to buy it. Instead of hunting for it every Christmas and crossing your fingers, you have it set aside in your name.

It's what many call "the subscribers' luck": not because they win more —the odds are the same for everyone—, but because they never end up without their number. That's the real advantage: the peace of mind of having it secured.

How to secure your number every year

 

The idea is simple: you reserve your number and, each year, it's kept for you to renew before it goes on general sale. That way you avoid the last-minute panic and the risk of someone getting ahead of you.

The details —when it's renewed, how it's confirmed, the deadline you have to decide— vary by administration. That's why it's best to ask about the specific conditions when you make your reservation, so you know exactly how and when to secure your number.

And if you give it up one year?

 

If you decide not to renew your subscription, the number will usually become free and can pass to someone else. It's not automatic or final everywhere, but it's worth being clear about: letting go of the subscription can mean losing your priority over that number in the future.

Since this depends on each administration's conditions, confirm it before you decide. If your number has sentimental value, it's worth knowing exactly what happens if you let it go one year.
