What is not legal —or safe— is buying on resale websites or on pages that are not official administrations. That is where the markups and the problems appear.

The requirements for buying online

 

To buy the Christmas lottery online with full guarantees, you normally need to meet these points:

→ Buy from an authorized administration: the website must belong to an official point of sale, integrated into the sales network. This is the most important requirement.

→ Be of legal age (+18): gambling is reserved for adults. Selling to minors is prohibited.

→ Register and identify yourself: an account is created with your details so that your purchase is in your name.

→ Pass the age and identity verification: the platform confirms that you are who you say you are and that you are of legal age.

The specific steps (what details are requested, how it is verified) may vary depending on the platform, so it is a good idea to confirm them when you register on the website. The underlying idea is always the same: an identified purchase, in your name and only for those of legal age.
