La Primitiva is one of the most played lotteries in Spain, but its days confuse a lot of people. Is it on Thursdays? On Saturdays? Is there a draw on Tuesdays? Let's clear it up once and for all.

The short answer: La Primitiva is drawn on Thursday and Saturday. There is no Primitiva on Tuesday (that day is EuroMillions). And on Sundays El Gordo de la Primitiva is drawn, which is a separate game.

We explain it in detail, with the cut-off (closing) schedule and how not to miss a draw by failing to validate in time.

In brief

Thursday and Saturday (not Tuesday)

La Primitiva is drawn on Thursday and Saturday. On Tuesday and Friday there is no Primitiva: those days it is EuroMillions. And on Sundays the Gordo de la Primitiva is drawn, a separate game. To take part, your bet must be validated before the cut-off (closing) of that day's draw.

What days La Primitiva is drawn

 

La Primitiva has two draws a week: Thursday and Saturday. It doesn't change from one week to the next: it is always those two days. This is the calendar for the La Primitiva family:

There is no Primitiva on Tuesday and Friday: those days EuroMillions is drawn. Always check the current days and schedules, which are set by Loterías y Apuestas del Estado (SELAE).

Is there a La Primitiva draw on Tuesdays?

 

No. This is the most common confusion, and the answer is clear: La Primitiva is not drawn on Tuesdays. What is drawn on Tuesdays (and Fridays) is EuroMillions, which is a different lottery, with its own rules and prizes.

So, if you were looking for 'the Tuesday Primitiva', you were actually looking for EuroMillions. La Primitiva, remember, is Thursday and Saturday. And yes: Saturday is one of its two days, in case that was your question.

El Gordo de la Primitiva: on Sundays

 

Watch out: 'Primitiva' and 'Gordo de la Primitiva' are not the same thing. El Gordo de la Primitiva is a separate game, with its own mechanics, drawn on Sundays.

That's why, when you plan your betting week, keep it in mind: La Primitiva on Thursdays and Saturdays; El Gordo de la Primitiva on Sundays. They are two different events, even if they share a surname.

Until what time can you place a bet

 

To enter a draw, your bet has to be paid for and validated before the cut-off (closing) of that day (Thursday or Saturday). The exact cut-off time is set by the channel or platform where you play, and you'll see it shown when you place the bet.

The practical rule is simple: don't leave it until the last minute. If you try to place a bet once the draw has closed, your bet won't enter it (at most, it will be registered for the next one). Validating with time to spare is the best way not to miss out.

Frequently asked questions about the days and schedule of La Primitiva

 
What days is La Primitiva drawn?

La Primitiva is drawn on Thursday and Saturday, every week. Those are its only two days. There is no Primitiva on Tuesday and Friday (those days it's EuroMillions), and on Sundays El Gordo de la Primitiva is drawn, which is a separate game.

Is there a La Primitiva draw on Tuesdays?

No. La Primitiva is not drawn on Tuesdays: that day (and Friday too) EuroMillions is drawn, which is a different lottery. It's a very common confusion. La Primitiva is always Thursday and Saturday.

Is La Primitiva drawn on Saturdays?

Yes. Saturday is one of La Primitiva's two draw days; the other is Thursday. So 'today's Saturday Primitiva' does exist: Saturday is a Primitiva day.

What is El Gordo de la Primitiva and when is it drawn?

It is a separate game from La Primitiva, with its own mechanics and prizes. It is drawn on Sundays. It shares a surname with La Primitiva, but they are two different lotteries on different days.

Until what time can I place a bet or validate La Primitiva?

Your bet must be validated before the draw's cut-off (closing), on the same day (Thursday or Saturday). The exact time is shown by the channel or platform where you play. The recommendation is not to wait until the last moment: if you place a bet once the draw has closed, it won't enter it.
