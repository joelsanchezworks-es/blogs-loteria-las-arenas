Which is easier to win?

 

If we are talking about the jackpot, La Primitiva is clearly easier. Matching its 6 numbers has a probability of 1 in 13,983,816; the EuroMillions jackpot (5 numbers and 2 stars) is 1 in 139,838,160. In other words, the La Primitiva jackpot is about 10 times more likely.

But 'easier' does not mean 'easy': we are talking about one in almost 14 million. It is worth being clear about that and playing for fun, never expecting to win. Both games also hand out smaller prizes for matching fewer numbers, with far higher odds than those of the jackpot.

Which has the better jackpot?

 

Here EuroMillions wins, and by a lot. Its jackpots start high and can grow up to a cap of €240 million, figures La Primitiva does not reach.

La Primitiva hands out jackpots of tens of millions, more modest but nothing to scoff at, and it also has its own added appeal: the Joker, an optional extra game with separate prizes. Better odds (La Primitiva) or a bigger jackpot (EuroMillions): that is the real choice.

Price and draws: the practical side

 

La Primitiva is cheaper: €1 per bet, versus EuroMillions' €2.50. If you play regularly, that difference counts, and it is another point in La Primitiva's favour for anyone looking to play little by little.

As for the days, they do not compete: La Primitiva is drawn on Thursday and Saturday and EuroMillions on Tuesday and Friday. In fact, many people play both, each on its own day. You can play EuroMillions online just as you do La Primitiva.

So, which one is right for me?

 

There is no 'right' answer: it depends on what you are after. If you prefer better odds of landing the jackpot and playing more cheaply, La Primitiva fits better. If a giant jackpot tempts you even though it is harder, EuroMillions is for you.

Whatever your choice, the healthy rule is the same: play responsibly, spend only what you want to put towards the fun and do not count on winning. You can play La Primitiva online whenever you like, with every guarantee.

Frequently asked questions about La Primitiva and EuroMillions

 
Which is easier to win, La Primitiva or EuroMillions?

The La Primitiva jackpot is easier: its probability is 1 in 13.98 million, versus 1 in 139.8 million for EuroMillions, about 10 times harder. That said, 'easier' is not 'easy': both are very unlikely and it is best to play without expecting to win.

Which has the bigger jackpot?

EuroMillions, by far. Its jackpots can grow up to a cap of €240 million. La Primitiva hands out jackpots of tens of millions: more modest, but more likely to be matched. It is the classic dilemma between better odds and a bigger prize.

Can I improve my odds of winning?

Which one is right for me?

It depends on what you are after: La Primitiva if you prefer better odds and cheaper play; EuroMillions if a much bigger jackpot appeals to you even though it is harder. There is no option that is better than the other in absolute terms: choose according to your preferences and play responsibly.
