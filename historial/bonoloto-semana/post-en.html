What days is Bonoloto drawn?

 

Bonoloto is drawn all seven days of the week, Monday to Sunday. Since September 2022 there's also a draw on Sundays, so it no longer takes a day off: you always have a draw that same night.

Playing every day or concentrating your bet: which is worth it?

 

There's no one answer that's better than the other here: it depends on how you want to enjoy it and on your budget. Playing continuously (the same bet every day) spreads out the cost and gives you a daily draw for €3.50 a week. Concentrating your bet on a single draw with several combinations —the multiple bet— gives you more options that day, but costs more.

→ Playing continuously: €0.50 a day, your number in all 7 draws. Ideal if you like routine and a tight budget.

→ Concentrating (multiple bet): more combinations in one draw, more chances that day, but the cost rises quickly.

In either case, the sensible thing is to set a limit and not go beyond it: playing more doesn't change the fact that the odds of the big prizes are low.

The multiple bet: more combinations, more cost

The multiple bet lets you mark more than 6 numbers, which generates several combinations in the same draw: more chances of winning that day, but the price goes up fast with each combination. Before you dive in, check how much a multiple Bonoloto bet costs and set yourself a limit.
