How to reserve a Christmas Lottery number for a company

How to hold your Christmas number without any confusion: what to prepare for the request, how the enquiry, the provisional hold and the purchase differ, and what you should agree before announcing anything to your team.

To reserve a Christmas Lottery number for your company, have your number preference, the approximate quantity of décimos (lottery tickets) and a contact person ready. At Lotería Las Arenas we check availability and confirm the provisional hold before preparing the campaign.

The important distinction is this: requesting a number does not mean it is reserved, and a reservation does not mean that every employee has already bought their décimo. These are separate moments and are best communicated separately. If you like, start with our Christmas Lottery for companies service and we will look at it with your particular case in mind.

The key distinction

Enquiring, holding and buying are three separate moments

The enquiry sets out what you need; the provisional hold is the confirmation we give you once availability has been checked, together with its conditions; and the purchase is the transaction through which the décimos are acquired. Having sent a preference does not mean the number is already reserved, and a reservation does not mean the staff have already bought. Communicate each step separately and take nothing for granted until you receive our confirmation.

Prepare a specific request

 

State the company name, the person coordinating, the number of décimos you expect to need and whether you are looking for an exact number or an ending. If you have not decided yet, you can ask for a proposal of available numbers.

It also helps to explain whether each employee will buy individually or whether the company will place a joint order. When there are several sites or physical delivery is required, mentioning it at the outset avoids setting up a campaign that later does not fit.

There is no need to turn the first enquiry into a list of personal details for the entire staff. To begin with, the organisational information and an approximate quantity are enough to assess the request.

→ Company name and the contact person who will coordinate the campaign.

→ Approximate quantity of décimos you expect to need.

→ Number preference: an exact number, an ending or a proposal of available numbers.

→ How the team buys: each employee individually or a joint order placed by the company.

→ Organisational notes: if there are several sites or physical delivery is required, say so at the outset.

Can you keep the number from previous years?

 

You can request it, but it should not be assumed to be renewed without confirmation. Having played a number in a previous campaign does not in itself prove that there is availability for this year's quantity.

Availability has to match both the number and the volume requested. If one of the two is not possible, other preferences can be considered before announcing the campaign to the team.

Choosing an ending for tradition's sake or because of a special date is a perfectly legitimate personal preference. That said, do not present it to the staff as an advantage for winning a prize, because all numbers have the same odds.

Distinguish between enquiry, hold and purchase

 

This distinction is not just ours: it matters inside the company too. An employee who has answered an interest survey should not take it to mean they have already bought, and the organiser should not announce a reserved quantity before receiving confirmation. Keep the record of what was agreed so that everyone in charge works from the same information; this is especially useful when tasks are shared between HR, administration and several sites.

The provisional hold is the step that confirms the reservation. Until you receive it, the request remains only an enquiry.

What you should agree in advance

 

Before the launch, check the number and the draw, the quantity reserved, how long the hold stays valid and the purchase methods planned. Clarify too how the purchases will be made and what will happen when the closing date arrives.

Do not take for granted that the reservation holds until the day of the draw, that it can be extended without limit or that unsold décimos can be returned. These conditions must be settled in the specific agreement.

If your forecast changes, check with us before announcing an extension. New interested parties appearing does not mean there are more décimos of the same number available for your campaign.

→ Number and draw confirmed for the campaign.

→ Quantity reserved and how long the hold is valid (until when it stays in place).

→ Purchase methods planned and how the purchases will be made.

→ Closing date and what happens when it arrives.

→ Returns and extensions: only those that have been agreed, without taking them for granted.

From the reserved number to the purchase space

 

Once the campaign is finalised, at Las Arenas we prepare the platform, the password, the poster and the instructions according to the method agreed. That is when the final information should be distributed to the team, and not before.

The reservation and the delivery method are not interchangeable. If an employee buys with an online deposit, their décimos are held in safekeeping; they are not making a purchase to collect them later. If they need physical delivery, they must choose an option set up for it.

An example of a first enquiry. You can write us something as simple as: «We are a company with two sites. We would like to check availability of our usual number for around 150 décimos. We would like each employee to buy online and to look at the delivery options». It is an illustrative example, not a recommended quantity or a real reservation: its value is that it lets us review the number, the quantity and the organisation without promising anything to the team too soon.
