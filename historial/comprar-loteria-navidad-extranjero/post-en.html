Being far from home doesn't mean missing out on the magic of Christmas. If you live abroad —or if you're playing from another country—, you can buy your Christmas Lottery just as if you were in Spain.

The key is to forget about paper: the most convenient and secure way from abroad is to buy online and leave your décimo in safekeeping (custody), without depending on any shipping. That way your décimo is stored, checked and in your name.

Let's look at the options, how the prize is collected from abroad and how to keep playing with your loved ones. This is general information, not tax or legal advice.

In brief

Online and in safekeeping: the simplest way from abroad

From abroad, the most practical option is to buy online on an official lottery office's website and leave your décimo in safekeeping (custody): you don't need it shipped to you, it stays stored and checked, and if there's a prize they let you know. International shipping of the physical décimo depends on each lottery office and country, so, if you want it in your hands, it's best to confirm availability first.

The simplest way: online and in safekeeping

 

By buying online on an official lottery office's website, your décimo is official, it's in your name and doesn't depend on a slip of paper travelling. By leaving it in safekeeping (custody), the lottery office stores and checks it for you: if there's a prize, they let you know, wherever you are.

It's the option designed precisely for those who are far away: no shipping, no risk of loss and no need to keep an eye on the result. You buy, you stop worrying and, if luck strikes, you find out.

And if I want the physical décimo? Shipping

 

If you'd rather have the décimo in your hands —to give as a gift or out of tradition—, a shipping option may exist… but don't take it for granted. International shipping depends on each lottery office and the destination country, so the first thing is to confirm whether it's available for yours.

For the detail on how shipping works, you can check our information on Christmas Lottery shipping and on lottery shipping. If shipping to your country isn't feasible, safekeeping (custody) remains the perfect alternative.

How the prize is collected if you live abroad

 

This is where buying online makes it easy for you again. If you left your décimo in safekeeping (custody), the lottery office checks it and lets you know if there's a prize; since the purchase is in your name, you don't depend on keeping a slip of paper or on being in Spain that day.

Prize payment follows the official procedure in Spain: the highest amounts are collected through authorised banks, with identification. If you live abroad, there may be particularities (for example, in non-residents' taxation), so it's best to check your specific case with the lottery office or an adviser. This is general information.

Playing with your family even when you're far away

 

For many emigrants, the Christmas Lottery is a thread that keeps them connected to home. You can keep playing the usual number with your family even if you're thousands of kilometres away: all it takes is buying it online or coordinating to share it.

If you play as a pool (peña) or share a décimo, put in writing who takes part and for how much, and keep a copy or photo of the décimo (or its receipt). That way, wherever you are, the split is clear from the start.

Can I buy the Christmas Lottery from abroad?

Yes, as long as you do it on the official website of a certified lottery office. The décimo is official, the price is the official one (€20) and the purchase is registered in your name. Wherever you buy from, the risk isn't 'online', but doing it on unauthorised websites.
