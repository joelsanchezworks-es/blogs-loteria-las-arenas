The great advantage of Bonoloto is that it starts paying out prizes very early: from just 3 matches. That is why it is one of the favourite daily lotteries, even though its jackpots are more modest than those of other games.

In Bonoloto you choose 6 numbers from 1 to 49. In each draw 6 numbers are drawn, plus a bonus number and a reintegro, and the prize tiers come from there. Let's look at them one by one, with single bet examples.

In brief

There's a prize from 3 matches

Bonoloto has five prize tiers by number of matches (from 6 to 3) plus the reintegro. With 3 matches there's already a prize: the 5th tier, a fixed prize of €4. From there, 4, 5, 5 + bonus number and 6 matches pay out prizes that vary by draw. The reintegro gives you your stake back (€0.50). And 2 matches win no prize.

What you win with 3, 4, 5 and 6 matches

 

With a single bet (€0.50), this is what each result means:

→ 3 matches (5th tier): a fixed prize of €4. It's the most common prize and doesn't depend on the draw.

→ 4 matches (4th tier): a variable prize, usually a few tens of euros depending on the draw.

→ 5 matches (3rd tier): a larger variable prize, which depends on how many winners there are.

→ 5 + bonus number (2nd tier): a higher tier, also variable, for anyone who matches 5 and, in addition, the bonus number.

→ 6 matches (1st tier): the top prize, the jackpot, which can roll over from one draw to the next.

As you can see, matching more numbers doesn't just move you up a tier: in the higher tiers the prize depends on the draw, so the exact amount is known after each one is held.

The bonus number and the reintegro: what they do

 

They are two extra numbers in the draw, and each one plays a very specific role:

→ The bonus number only counts for the 2nd tier: it rewards anyone who matches 5 of the 6 numbers and, in addition, that bonus number (what is known as '5 + bonus number'). For the other tiers it has no effect.

→ The reintegro is a single digit (from 0 to 9). If the one on your ticket matches the one in the draw, you get your stake back (€0.50). It's the smallest 'prize' and the most frequent, and it's independent of the numbers you match.

And with 2 matches? The most common question

 

It's the big question, and the answer is clear: 2 matches win no prize in Bonoloto. The prize for matched numbers starts at the 5th tier, with 3 matches. Matching 1 or 2 numbers doesn't entitle you to anything.

That said, there's a nuance: the reintegro is drawn separately. Even if you match no numbers (or just 1 or 2), if your reintegro matches the one in the draw, you get your €0.50 stake back. It's not a prize for matches, but it does give you back what you staked.

Frequently asked questions about Bonoloto prizes

 
How many matches do you need to win Bonoloto?

The prize for numbers starts at 3 matches (5th tier, a fixed prize of €4). From there: 4 matches (4th), 5 matches (3rd), 5 + bonus number (2nd) and 6 matches (1st tier, the top prize). In addition, matching the reintegro —which is drawn separately— gives you your stake back even if you match no numbers.

What do you win with 6 matches in Bonoloto?

With 6 matches you win the 1st tier, the top prize in Bonoloto (the jackpot). Its amount is variable: it depends on the takings and the number of winners, and it can roll over from one draw to the next if there are no winners. The exact amount is known after each draw.

Is there a prize with 2 matches in Bonoloto?

No. The prize for matched numbers starts at 3 matches (5th tier). With 1 or 2 matches there's no prize. The only thing that can give you money back in that case is the reintegro: if the digit on your ticket matches the one in the draw, you get your €0.50 stake back, regardless of the numbers you've matched.

What is the bonus number for?

The bonus number only counts for the 2nd tier: it rewards anyone who matches 5 of the 6 numbers and, in addition, the bonus number ('5 + bonus number'). For the other tiers it has no effect: only your matches among the 6 main numbers count.

What is the reintegro and how much do you win?

The reintegro (refund number) is a single digit (from 0 to 9) drawn separately. If the one on your ticket matches the one in the draw, you get your stake back (€0.50 on a single bet). It's the smallest prize but the most frequent, and it doesn't depend on how many numbers you match.

Are Bonoloto prizes always the same?

Not all of them. The 5th tier (3 matches) is a fixed prize of €4 and the reintegro always gives your stake back (€0.50). The 1st to 4th tiers, however, are variable: they depend on the takings and the winners in each draw. That's why it's worth checking the official details of each draw for the exact amount.
