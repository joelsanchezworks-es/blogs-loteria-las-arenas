A number with a history

 

The superdécimo box is not just any décimo (a lottery ticket): it comes from a number with a history. In last year's Christmas Draw, Lotería Las Arenas had the joy of awarding a third prize from its lottery office in the Arenas shopping centre, and this year that number returns as the star of a special edition.

The idea is simple: to give that number the format it deserves. Rather than handing it over as a loose décimo, the office presents it inside a carefully crafted box, designed both for those who want to play it and for those looking for a different kind of gift this festive season.

It is worth remembering, however, that in the lottery all numbers have exactly the same probability. The superdécimo box is a special format and a nod to last year's good fortune, not a guarantee of a prize: what it offers is excitement, a carefully crafted presentation and the convenience of reserving it online.

What makes the superdécimo box special

 

It is a limited-edition presentation that turns buying your décimo into something more special. Here is what sets it apart:

→ Limited edition: a limited number of units, available while stocks last.

→ Gift presentation: a carefully crafted box, ideal for surprising family, friends or clients.

→ A number with a history: built around the number that awarded a third prize last year.

→ Online or in-person reservation: it can be ordered from the superdécimo page or at our lottery office in the Arenas shopping centre.

→ Official, fully guaranteed play: within an Official Lottery Office of the State National Lottery.

Frequently asked questions about the superdécimo box

 
What is the superdécimo box?

It is a limited edition from Lotería Las Arenas: a special, boxed presentation of the Christmas décimo, built around the number with which the office awarded a third prize in last year's Christmas Draw. It is designed to be played, given as a gift or collected.

Does buying this number increase the chances of winning?

No. In the lottery all numbers have exactly the same probability of winning a prize, regardless of whether they have won in the past. The superdécimo box is a special format and a nod to the previous year's good fortune, not a guarantee of a prize. What it offers is excitement, a carefully crafted presentation and the convenience of reserving it online.

Where can I get the superdécimo box?

In two ways: online, by reserving it on the superdécimo Christmas page, or in person, at the Lotería Las Arenas office in the Arenas shopping centre in Barcelona (Gran Via de les Corts Catalanes, 373-385).

How long is it available for?

It is a limited edition, so it is available while stocks last. The Christmas Draw takes place on 22 December 2026, but units may run out well before that date. That is why we recommend reserving it early.

Can it be given as a gift?

Yes, in fact that is precisely what it is designed for. The box format turns the décimo into a more striking gift: it works for Secret Santa, for clients, for syndicates and groups, or as a gesture at home. Companies wishing to offer a corporate gift can write to empresas@loterialasarenas.com.

Is it official lottery?

Yes. Lotería Las Arenas is Official Lottery Office no. 336 of the State National Lottery, in the Arenas shopping centre in Barcelona. The superdécimo box contains official lottery, with all the guarantees of regulated play.
