A company with several sites can share a single Christmas Lottery campaign without organising every purchase and delivery in the same way. The starting point is common communication and options agreed in advance for the team's needs.

At Lotería Las Arenas we can set up a private virtual space for the company and look into the deposit, individual delivery, group delivery or in-person purchase options that apply. Having several sites does not mean that every destination or service is enabled automatically. You can start with our company lottery service.

The key point

A common foundation, with delivery differences kept clearly separate

All participants share the same draw, number, access and general conditions. What changes —and must be explained separately— is the delivery: one site may receive a group delivery and another may not. With several sites and remote work, the important thing is to map out the team's real organisation first and then tell each person what applies to them, without contradictory instructions.

Map out the team's real organisation first

 

Before requesting the campaign, distinguish between those who usually work at a site, those who come in some days and those who work remotely. Also note whether any sites have shifts or closures that affect the receipt of a delivery.

There is no need to start by sharing the personal addresses of the whole staff. To look into the organisation, it is useful to know the general locations, the approximate quantities and whether or not physical delivery is needed.

If there are workers outside Spain, ask about their case specifically. International delivery must not be promised, nor the ability to buy from any country, without confirmation.

Shared information, without contradictory instructions

 

Appoint a main point of contact and, where necessary, a coordination lead at each site. We recommend that everyone uses a single base communication with the same draw, number, access and general campaign conditions.

Delivery differences must be explained separately. If one site receives a group delivery and another does not, forwarding the same announcement without clarification is not enough.

It is also advisable to communicate any changes to all affected locations. An updated poster at the main site does not on its own correct an old instruction still circulating among the remote workers.

Online deposit for those who do not need physical delivery

 

Buying online with deposit leaves the décimos (lottery tickets) in safekeeping at our lottery office. It may be an option to consider when the worker does not wish to receive them physically.

It does not involve subsequent collection, delivery to a site or any automatic dispatch. The worker should understand this before buying and not assume that they will be able to collect the décimos when they next return to Barcelona.

The online space and the campaign conditions should be the reference point for that purchase, not the system used by another company.

Home delivery or group delivery: choose according to the case

 

When individual delivery is enabled, each order must comply with the permitted destinations and conditions. Before announcing it as a solution for the whole team, check coverage, costs and organisation.

Group delivery to the company requires confirming the destination and someone responsible for receiving it. If you need deliveries to several sites, raise this explicitly: a group-delivery agreement should not be taken to include any number of deliveries.

At each location that receives décimos, the corresponding distribution must be coordinated. The list of buyers helps with this task, but does not replace the person who has to carry it out.

Agree a cut-off that leaves no delivery without someone responsible

 

Review the purchase cut-off and the agreed delivery dates, bearing in mind any expected absences. As an organising principle, it is better to settle who will receive each delivery before publicising the campaign.

Do not copy dates from another site or another year. If an option has a different deadline, explain it without mixing it up with the general draw date.

An example of a hybrid campaign. Imagine an organisation with a main office and a remote team: the person responsible looks into a campaign with online purchase and considers a group delivery for the office, along with other options for those who do not come in to the office. Before communicating it, they confirm which options are feasible and the conditions of each; they then make clear in the notice what applies to each worker and who will handle any questions. It is a coordination approach, not a service package included for all companies, and it does not assume multiple deliveries at no cost.

Frequently asked questions about the company lottery with several sites

 
Can a single campaign be run for several sites?

Yes. A company with several sites can share one campaign without organising every purchase and delivery in the same way. The foundation is common communication (same draw, number, access and general conditions) and agreed options for each group's needs. What changes between sites —above all the delivery— is explained separately.

Does having several sites automatically enable all deliveries?

No. Having several sites does not mean that every destination or service is enabled by default. A group-delivery agreement does not include any number of deliveries: if you need deliveries to several locations, you must raise this explicitly and check coverage, costs and organisation before announcing it.

How do I organise the lottery for remote workers?

For those who do not need the paper décimo, buying online with deposit (safekeeping at the lottery office) usually fits well: it does not involve subsequent collection, delivery to a site or automatic dispatch. Anyone who wants to receive it physically can use home delivery, when available, checking its destinations, costs and conditions. The important thing is that each worker understands their option before buying.

Can a remote worker collect their décimo when they pass through Barcelona?

Only if they have chosen a delivery option that allows it. With online deposit, the décimos are kept in safekeeping and that purchase is not a deferred collection: it should not be assumed that they can be collected on the next trip to Barcelona. If someone expects to want the décimo in hand, they should choose an enabled delivery option from the outset, with its conditions.

How do I prevent contradictory instructions from circulating between sites?

With a common base communication for everyone and the delivery differences explained separately. It helps to appoint a main point of contact and, if needed, someone responsible per site, and to communicate any change to all affected locations. A poster updated only at the main site does not correct an old instruction still circulating among the remote workers.

Do you offer international delivery for employees outside Spain?

It should not be taken for granted. If you have workers outside Spain, you must ask about their case specifically: international delivery and the ability to buy from any country are not promised without confirmation. If you tell us about these situations when setting up the campaign, we will look into what is feasible for each one.
