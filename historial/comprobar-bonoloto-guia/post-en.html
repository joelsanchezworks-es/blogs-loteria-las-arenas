Checking the Bonoloto is quick, but it is worth doing it properly so you don't miss out on any prize. The Bonoloto is drawn Monday to Saturday, so if you play on several days, you have to check each draw separately.

There are three ways to do it: by number (comparing your ticket with the results), by QR (scanning it) and online with a checker. The easiest and safest is the official checker, which tells you instantly whether your ticket —physical or online— has won a prize.

You can check your Bonoloto in a moment. And below we explain how to interpret the result.

In brief

Three ways to check your Bonoloto

You can check it by number (compare your 6 numbers with the 6 that came up, plus the bonus number (complementario) and the refund (reintegro)), by QR (scan the ticket) or with the online checker (the quickest way, valid for physical and online tickets). Remember: the Bonoloto is played Monday to Saturday, so check each day's draw.

Checking the Bonoloto by number, step by step

 

In the Bonoloto you choose 6 numbers from 1 to 49. In the draw, 6 numbers are drawn, plus a bonus number (complementario) and a refund (reintegro). To check your ticket by hand, compare your numbers with the official results of the draw you are playing:

1

Find the draw results

Look at the 6 winning numbers, the bonus number (complementario) and the refund (reintegro) for that day's draw (the Bonoloto is drawn Monday to Saturday).

2

Count your matches

Check how many of your 6 numbers match the 6 drawn. The order doesn't matter.

3

Check the bonus number (complementario)

It only counts if you have 5 matches: 5 + bonus number (complementario) is the 2nd category.

4

Check the refund (reintegro)

If your refund (reintegro) matches the one from the draw, you get back the cost of the bet (0,50 €).

5

Identify your category

Based on your matches, you'll know which prize category you're in (we look at this in the table below).

Checking the Bonoloto by QR

 

If your ticket is physical, it carries a QR code you can scan to check it instantly. Just open the checker (the official app or the lottery office's online checker) and point the camera at the QR on the ticket.

It is the easiest way to avoid mistakes when comparing numbers by hand, especially if you play several bets or several draws. The QR identifies your ticket and shows you directly whether there is a prize and which category.

Checking the Bonoloto online: the quickest way

 

The online checker is the easiest option, and it works whether your ticket is physical or you play online. With the Bonoloto checker you enter your numbers or scan the QR and see the draw result straight away.

If you play online, your bets are registered in your name and, in many cases, checked automatically: you're notified if there's a prize. Even so, checking the official result is always the way to be sure, including for checking today's Bonoloto.

What the refund (reintegro) and the bonus number (complementario) mean

 

They are two extra numbers from the draw, and each one serves a different purpose:

→ The bonus number (complementario) is an additional number that only counts towards the 2nd category: it rewards anyone who matches 5 numbers plus the bonus number (complementario) (5 + C).

→ The refund (reintegro) is a single digit (from 0 to 9). If the one on your ticket matches the one from the draw, you get back the cost of the bet (0,50 €). It is the smallest prize, but the most frequent.

The weekly bet: checking several draws

 

The Bonoloto is drawn Monday to Saturday, so you can play the same bet for several draws in the week with a single ticket. In that case, each draw is independent: you have to check your ticket draw by draw, one for each day you play.

The checker makes this task much easier: with the QR or with your numbers you can review each draw without getting confused. Don't write off a whole week without having checked all its draws.

Have you won? What to do if there's a prize

 

If your ticket has won, the first thing is to keep it safe. If it is physical, store it without folding or damaging it and, if you like, sign the back: it is your proof of the prize.

Where to claim depends on the amount: prizes of up to 2.000 € per ticket are paid at points of sale and lottery offices; higher amounts, at authorised banks, with your ID document. And mind the deadline: the right to claim expires after 3 months from the day after the draw.

If you played online, the prize is handled according to your account terms. Not playing online yet? You can play Bonoloto online and not have to worry about losing the ticket or forgetting to check.

Frequently asked questions about checking the Bonoloto

 
How do I check today's Bonoloto?

The quickest way is the online checker: you enter your numbers or scan the ticket's QR and see the result of that day's draw instantly. You can also check it by hand, comparing your 6 numbers with the 6 that came up, plus the bonus number (complementario) and the refund (reintegro). Remember that the Bonoloto is drawn Monday to Saturday, so each day there's a different draw to check.

How do you check the Bonoloto by QR?

Physical tickets carry a QR code. Open the checker (the official app or the lottery office's online checker), point the camera at the QR on the ticket and the system identifies your ticket and tells you straight away whether it has won a prize and which category. It is the easiest way to avoid mistakes when comparing numbers by hand.

What is the refund (reintegro) in the Bonoloto?

The refund (reintegro) is a single digit (from 0 to 9) drawn separately. If the refund (reintegro) on your ticket matches the one from the draw, you get back the cost of the bet (0,50 €). It is the smallest prize in the Bonoloto, but also the most frequent.

What is the bonus number (complementario) for?

The bonus number (complementario) is an additional number that only counts towards the 2nd category: it rewards anyone who matches 5 of the 6 numbers plus the bonus number (complementario) (known as “5 + bonus number (complementario)”). For the other categories it has no effect: only your matches among the 6 main numbers count.

How many matches do you need to win?

In the Bonoloto there's a prize from 3 matches (5th category, a fixed prize of 4 €). From there: 4 matches (4th), 5 matches (3rd), 5 + bonus number (complementario) (2nd) and the 6 numbers (1st category, the biggest prize). In addition, matching the refund (reintegro) returns the cost of the bet, even if you don't match any number.

Where and until when can you claim a Bonoloto prize?

Prizes of up to 2.000 € per ticket are paid at points of sale and lottery offices; higher amounts, at authorised banks, with your ID document. The right to claim expires after 3 months from the day after the draw, so it's best to check and claim as soon as possible. If you play online, the prize is handled according to your account terms.
