How many décimos and how many series are there?

 

In 2026 there are 205 million décimos on sale. The maths works out like this: the draw has 100,000 numbers (from 00000 to 99999), and several 'series' are printed of each number. This year there are 205 series, and since each series is 100,000 numbers split into 10 décimos, that gives those 205 million décimos.

That's why the same number can be played by many people at once in different cities: there isn't just one 79,432, there are 205 'copies' of each number. And all of them, we stress, with exactly the same probability.

Frequently asked questions about the Christmas Lottery figures

 
How much money does the Christmas Lottery 2026 pay out?

€2,870 million in prizes, €98 million more than in 2025. That's 70% of a total of €4,100 million issued. These are official SELAE figures (it's worth confirming them in their announcement).

How many décimos and series are there this year?

205 series and, therefore, 205 million décimos. The draw has 100,000 numbers and each series is 100,000 numbers in 10 décimos, so the same number is played in 205 'copies' at once.

How much does el Gordo pay out at Christmas?

€400,000 per décimo (€4 million per series). It's the first prize and hasn't changed from previous years. Remember that, above €40,000, the Spanish tax office (Hacienda) applies a withholding.

How many prizes are there in total?

Thousands of prizes are paid out per series: el Gordo, 2nd and 3rd prizes, 2 fourth prizes, 8 fifth prizes, 1,794 in la pedrea and, on top of that, approximations, hundreds, ending-digit prizes and refunds. 70% of what's issued comes back in prizes.

Why are there several décimos with the same number?

Because several series are printed of each number. In 2026 there are 205, so there are 205 décimos of each number, spread across Spain. They all play the same: all numbers have the same probability.

How much does a décimo cost and where do I buy it?

€20 (official price). You can buy it at an official lottery office, in person or online. We are Official Lottery Office no. 336, in the C.C. Arenas de Barcelona.
