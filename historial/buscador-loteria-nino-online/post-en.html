What the Lotería del Niño is and when it's played

 

The Lotería del Niño is the second big draw of the year, right after the Christmas one. It is held every 6 January, on Three Kings' Day, and for this edition the draw will be on 6 January 2027.

As at Christmas, it's played with décimos (lottery tickets) for a five-figure number, and the fun lies in landing on that number (or that ending) that means something to you. The difference is that many people leave it to the last minute… and the most sought-after numbers fly off the shelves. Searching online in good time is the best way not to miss out on yours.

Unusual endings and special numbers: how to choose

 

No number is luckier than another, but some numbers do mean more to you. Searching online is perfect for landing on those unusual endings that are hard to find over the counter:

→ Dates that mean something: a birthday, an anniversary, the year someone was born.

→ Particular endings: the last two digits you always play or want to try.

→ Palindrome numbers and quirky numbers: 12321, 45654… harder to find, easy to search for online.

→ Rarely requested endings: if the usual ones are sold out, the finder helps you track down alternatives.

An honest reminder: in the lottery all numbers have exactly the same probability. Choosing an ending is a matter of sentiment, not a strategy for winning. What you can make sure of is buying it the right way, with guarantees.

The Lotería del Niño Extraordinary Draw takes place on 6 January 2027, on Three Kings' Day. It's the second big draw of the year, after the Christmas one. It's worth searching for and reserving your number in good time, because the most sought-after endings sell out before that date.
