In brief

Cash yes, but up to a limit

Spanish law prohibits paying in cash for any operation of €1,000 or more when one of the parties acts as a business or professional. Below that figure, you can pay in cash. A €2,000 pack exceeds the limit, so it is paid by card, Bizum or bank transfer. General information, not legal or tax advice.

Can you pay for the lottery in cash?

 

It depends on the amount. Buying a décimo (€20) or a few in cash is no problem at all: these are amounts well below the legal limit.

Things change with large purchases. When the amount of the operation reaches €1,000, a rule comes into play that prohibits cash payment if one of the parties is a business. And a lottery administration is one.

The €1,000 limit, explained

 

Spanish law does not allow cash payment for operations of €1,000 or more in which one of the parties acts as a business or professional. It doesn't matter if you pay the rest another way: what counts is the total amount of the operation.

Two important details: a purchase cannot be split into several payments to dodge the limit (the total is added up), and breaking the rule carries sanctions (a fine on the amount paid in cash). This is general information; for a specific case, consult an adviser or ask us.

What about paying part in cash and the rest by card?

Within a single operation of €1,000 or more, no: cash is not accepted, not even partially. Since what counts is the total amount, a €2,000 pack cannot include any cash; it is paid in full by banking methods. Genuinely separate and independent operations, each with its own amount, are a different matter. Frequently asked questions about paying for the lottery in cash

 
Can you pay for the lottery in cash?

Yes, as long as the amount is below €1,000. A décimo (€20) or a small purchase is fine in cash. From €1,000 in an operation with a business, no.

Why can't I pay €1,000 or more in cash?

Because of a Spanish rule that limits cash payments when one of the parties acts as a business or professional. It aims to control fraud. The limit is €1,000 per operation.

Can I pay part in cash and the rest by card?

Within a single operation of €1,000 or more, no: cash is not accepted, not even partially, because the total amount counts. Nor can the purchase be split to dodge the limit.

So how do I pay for a €2,000 pack like the Superdécimo?

By card, Bizum or bank transfer. It's fast, secure and everything is registered in your name. No cash for that amount.

Are there any exceptions to the limit?

What happens if I go over the limit?

Paying in cash above the limit can carry a sanction on the amount paid in cash. It's not worth it: for large amounts, always use banking methods.
