Playing La Primitiva from your phone has a big advantage: you do it in a minute and your bet is registered in your name, with no paper ticket to lose. But there are three things worth being clear about: how to pay, the deadline to validate and where your receipt is.

La Primitiva is drawn on Thursday and Saturday, you pick 6 numbers from 1 to 49 and, if you like, you add the Joker. The rest — choosing, paying and keeping the ticket receipt — we cover in this guide.

You can play La Primitiva online from your phone in a few steps. Let's go through them.

In brief

Playing from your phone, in three key points

You pick your 6 numbers (or leave them to chance), pay by card or with the balance in your account and the bet is validated automatically: there's nothing to stamp. The receipt is digital and is saved in your account, in your name. That said, you have to complete the bet before the draw closes: if you don't make it, it goes into the next one.

How to play La Primitiva from your phone, step by step

 

The process is almost identical on phone and computer, but on your phone you always have it to hand. These are the steps:

1

Log in to your account

Go to the official website or app where you play online and sign in. Playing online requires a verified account (you are of legal age and a resident).

2

Choose La Primitiva

Select the game and the draw you want to enter (Thursday or Saturday). You can play one or several draws.

3

Mark your 6 numbers

Pick 6 numbers from 1 to 49 by tapping the screen, or press the random bet option to have them generated for you.

4

Add the Joker if you like

The Joker is an extra, optional game with its own prize. Turn it on only if you're interested; it isn't compulsory.

5

Pay for the bet

Confirm the amount and pay with your usual method. Each single bet on La Primitiva costs €1.

6

Done: automatic validation

Your bet is registered and validated instantly, in your name. You don't have to stamp or keep any paper ticket.

Which payment methods you can use

 

On your phone you pay at the very moment you place the bet. The methods depend on the official platform where you play, but the usual ones are:

→ Bank card (debit or credit): the most widespread method for playing online.

→ Balance or wallet of your gaming account: if you've topped up beforehand, the bet is deducted from there instantly.

→ Other methods depending on the platform: some offer additional options; you'll see them when you confirm the payment.

Whatever the method, the price of the bet is always the official one (€1 for a single bet on La Primitiva): playing online adds no surcharge just for doing it from your phone.

Until what time you can validate your bet

 

This is the part that raises the most questions, and the most important one. To take part in a draw, your bet has to be paid for and validated before the close of that draw, on the same day it's held (Thursday or Saturday).

The exact closing time is set by the official platform and you'll see it shown when you place the bet. The practical rule is simple: don't leave it until the last minute. If you try to play once the draw has closed, your bet won't go into that draw (at most, it can be registered for the next one).

Where your receipt ends up

 

When you play from your phone you don't get a paper ticket: your receipt is digital. The bet is saved in your account, registered in your name, and you can check it whenever you like from 'My bets' or the equivalent section.

This has a clear advantage over paper: you can't lose the ticket or forget where you left it. Even so, it's a good idea to check that the bet appears correctly registered right after you pay.

If your bet wins a prize

 

Playing online, claiming is simpler. Prizes are paid automatically into your account up to a certain amount; for the larger ones, the usual claim procedure with identification is followed. Since the bet is in your name, you don't depend on keeping a paper ticket.

Remember that the right to claim a La Primitiva prize expires after 3 months from the day after the draw, so it's worth checking your bets regularly. If you play from your phone, checking the results takes you just a few seconds.

Frequently asked questions about playing La Primitiva from your phone

 
How do you play La Primitiva from your phone?

You log in to your account on the official website or app, choose La Primitiva and the draw (Thursday or Saturday), mark 6 numbers from 1 to 49 (or leave them to chance), add the Joker if you like and pay. It depends on the official platform, but the usual way is to pay by bank card (debit or credit) or with the balance in your gaming account if you've topped it up beforehand.
