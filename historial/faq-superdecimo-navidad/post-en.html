Price

 

The essentials on how much it costs and why:

How much does the Christmas superdécimo cost?

The superdécimo includes an official décimo of the Christmas Lottery — whose base price is €20 — with the special superdécimo presentation. You'll always find the exact amount and what it includes, kept up to date, in the price of the 2026 Christmas Lottery superdécimo.

Why doesn't it cost the same as a standalone décimo?

Because it's a special edition: on top of the lottery ticket (which plays exactly the same as any décimo) comes its presentation, designed for gifting. The element of chance is identical; the extra is what makes it special. You'll find the breakdown on the price page.

Number

 

About which number you get and which draw it plays:

Can I choose the superdécimo's number?

It depends on availability at the time, because it's a limited edition. The best thing is to ask when you order and we'll tell you what's available. If you're after a specific number, the sooner you ask, the easier it'll be to get it.

Does it play the same draw as the Christmas Lottery?

Yes. The superdécimo plays the Christmas Lottery Draw on 22 December, just like any décimo, and with the same odds. It's not a separate draw: it's the same Christmas Lottery as always, with our presentation.

Can the superdécimo sell out?

Yes. Being a limited edition, there are only so many to go around and the most sought-after numbers fly off the shelves. If you've more or less made up your mind, it's better not to wait until the last minute: that way you secure yours (and, if it's a gift, it arrives in time).

Gift and delivery

 

If you want it as a gift, two common questions:

Is the superdécimo a good gift?

It's one of the most appreciated Christmas gifts: guaranteed excitement and a presentation designed for just that. An honest note: the lottery ticket is a bearer instrument (al portador), so when you give it as a gift it's worth making clear whose it is, to enjoy it without misunderstandings.

How do I collect or receive it?

The most direct way is in person, at our lottery office in C.C. Arenas de Barcelona. If you order online, you can choose from the pickup or delivery options available at the time; it's worth confirming timeframes when you place the order, especially if it's a gift with a deadline.

Payout

 

If it wins, here's what you need to know to collect it:

If it wins, who collects and how?

The lottery ticket is a bearer instrument (al portador): whoever presents the décimo collects. You can collect small prizes at a lottery office; larger amounts, through a bank. A tip: keep the décimo safe and take a photo of it.

Are prizes taxed?

Only above €40,000: below that, the prize is exempt and you collect it in full; above it, the Spanish tax office (Hacienda) withholds 20% on the excess. It's the general rule for state lotteries; it's worth confirming with SELAE.
