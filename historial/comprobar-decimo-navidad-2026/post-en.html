1. Check online (web)

 

It's the fastest way: you go to an official checker, type in your number and it tells you instantly whether it has won and how much. It doesn't depend on opening hours or queues, and you can do it from your phone while you watch the draw.

Our tip: have the number written down (or the décimo in front of you) so you don't get a digit wrong. A single digit changes the result completely.

2. Check with the code (QR) on the décimo

 

Every décimo carries a code that identifies your bet. With the official Loterías y Apuestas del Estado app you can scan it and check the prize without typing the number, which avoids mistakes when copying it.

It's very handy if you play several décimos: you scan them one by one. That said, always download the app from the official store to make sure it's the genuine one.

3. Check at the lottery office

 

The classic option: you take your décimo to a lottery office and they check it for you (and, if it's a smaller prize, they pay you right there). For many, it's part of the Christmas Day ritual.

Frequently asked questions about how to check your décimo for the 2026 Christmas Lottery

 
How do I check my décimo for the 2026 Christmas Lottery?

Three ways: online with an official checker (you type in the number), by scanning the code/QR on the décimo with the official Loterías app, or at the lottery office in person. All three are reliable; online is the fastest on draw day.

Can I check the décimo by scanning the QR?

Yes. Every décimo carries a code that you can scan with the official Loterías y Apuestas del Estado app to check the prize without typing the number. Always download the app from the official store.

Where do I claim the prize if I win?

Smaller prizes are paid at the lottery office; large amounts are paid through a bank, which asks you to identify yourself. Keep the décimo in good condition: it's the document that proves the prize.

From when can I check the décimo?

From the moment the result starts coming out on 22 December itself. During the draw the prizes are sung out one by one; with the online checker you'll know as soon as your number comes up (or when the draw ends).

Is it safe to check the lottery online?

Yes, as long as you use an official checker (that of an official lottery office or the Loterías app). To check, you only need the number: they will never ask for your bank details to tell you whether you've won. Be wary if they do.

What do I do if I lose the décimo?

The décimo is a bearer instrument (al portador), so it's worth looking after it and keeping a photo just in case. If you share it, make it clear who's keeping it. In a specific case, it's best to ask at the lottery office as soon as possible.
