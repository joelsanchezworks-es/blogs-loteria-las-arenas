The golden rule: don't leave it to the last minute

 

The most common mistake is ordering the gift in the last week, when everything gets tighter. The sooner you place the order, the more relaxed you'll be: you secure the number you want and leave room for delivery.

Think of the date in reverse: if you want it for Christmas Eve, count backwards with plenty of room, adding a day or two just in case. Ordering it early is, always, the safest way to make sure it arrives in time.

Delivery options: shipping, pick-up or safekeeping

 

You have more than one way to get your superdécimo, and not all of them depend on a last-minute shipment:

→ Home delivery: you receive it at home to give it in person. It's the most convenient option if you order it in good time.

→ Pick-up (collection) at the lottery office: you buy it and collect it yourself, without depending on shipping deadlines.

→ Safekeeping (custody): the lottery office keeps it and checks it; no shipping needed, although it's no good if what you want is to hand over the physical superdécimo as a gift.

For the details of how shipping works, you can check out Christmas Lottery shipping, lottery home delivery and lottery shipping.

What if you're short on time?

 

If you remembered late, don't panic: you still have options. The fastest is usually to buy it online and collect it at the lottery office, or to order shipping after first confirming the delivery time for your area.

And if even so it wouldn't arrive in time for Christmas Eve, you can always leave it in safekeeping and give the recipient the receipt or a card, with the superdécimo kept and in their name. What matters is that the excitement gets there; the ticket can wait a few days.

Frequently asked questions about the timing for receiving your superdécimo

 
How late can I order the superdécimo to receive it before Christmas?

The golden rule is to order it as early as possible, with time to spare before 24 December, and not to leave it until the last week. The exact shipping deadlines depend on logistics and are best confirmed when you place the order. The sooner you order it, the more certain it is to arrive in time.

What delivery options do I have?

Three main ones: home delivery (you receive it at home to give as a gift), pick-up at the lottery office (it doesn't depend on shipping deadlines) and safekeeping (the lottery office keeps and checks it, with no shipping). You'll find the details of shipping in our articles about lottery shipping.

Will it arrive before Christmas Eve?

If you order it with enough time to spare, yes. To make sure, confirm the shipping deadline for your area when you place the order and don't leave it until the 22nd or 23rd. If you're really short on time, picking it up at the lottery office is usually the fastest option.

What if I don't make it in time for shipping?

You have alternatives: pick it up yourself at the lottery office or leave it in safekeeping and give the recipient a card or the receipt, with the superdécimo kept in their name. Safekeeping needs no shipping, so it's the perfect solution if you're short on time.

Can the superdécimo sell out?

Yes. The superdécimo is a limited edition, with a limited number of units, so it's worth ordering it early to secure the number you want. It's one more reason, besides shipping, not to leave it to the last minute.
