Phrases for your partner

 

For the person who shares your days (and, with luck, the prize):

→ «Here's to luck being on our side this year… and every year we share.»

→ «I don't need a win to know I already hit the jackpot with you.»

→ «A little shared hope to carry us to 22 December. I love you.»

→ «If it wins, we celebrate; if it doesn't, we still do. What matters is dreaming it together.»

→ «Our safest bet is still the same as ever: you and me.»

Phrases for your parents and family

 

For the people who gave you everything, a little piece of hope:

→ «In case this is the year we all celebrate together. With all my love.»

→ «A little piece of hope for the family that gave me everything.»

→ «Thank you for everything. Here's hoping luck pays a little of it back.»

→ «May Christmas bring us health and, as a bonus, a little joy.»

→ «For you — you're the best prize I'll ever win.»

Phrases for friends

 

For your oldest friends, the ones you share everything with:

→ «We share the laughs, the plans, and now the hope too. And if it wins — let's party!»

→ «Here's to luck catching us together, like always.»

→ «A décimo's worth of hope to toast the season. Here's to us!»

→ «Because good friends share their luck too.»

→ «If the Gordo comes in, you already know who you're celebrating with.»

Phrases for workmates

 

For your Secret Santa or as a gift for the team:

→ «Here's to luck finding its way into the office this year too.»

→ «Sharing a bit of hope makes the team stronger. Good luck, everyone!»

→ «To a December full of good news… and maybe a surprise.»

→ «May we all win — we've certainly earned it.»

→ «A little something to wish you happy holidays, with an extra dose of hope.»

How to write the perfect card

 

You don't need to be a poet: one sincere line is worth more than an elaborate paragraph. Add the person's name, choose a tone that suits your relationship and sign off. If in doubt, keep it simple: «Happy holidays, [name]. Here's to luck being on our side.»

And an honest note for your message: the Christmas superdécimo is an official décimo in a limited edition, with the same prize and the same odds as any other décimo. What you're giving isn't «more luck», but a bit of excitement and a lovely presentation. So promise affection, not prizes.

Frequently asked questions about phrases for giving a superdécimo

 
What can I write to go with a Christmas superdécimo?

A short, sincere line, tailored to the person: romantic for your partner, affectionate for family, fun for friends and warm for workmates. In this article you'll find more than 20 examples sorted by recipient. Ideally, add the name of whoever receives it and sign off.

Are there phrases in Catalan as well as Spanish?

Yes. The printable card includes a version in both Spanish and Catalan, and the phrases in this article can easily be adapted to either language. Choose the one that best suits the person you're giving it to.

Can I print a card for the superdécimo?

Yes. At the end of the article you can download a ready-to-print card for free, designed to go with the superdécimo. Just print it, write your message and place it alongside the superdécimo.

Is a short line or a long message better?

Almost always, short. One line with the person's name and a sincere wish works better than a long text. If you like, add a personal touch (a memory, an inside joke of yours), but don't overdo it: the whole point is that it can be read at a glance.

Does the superdécimo bring more luck because it's special?

No. The superdécimo is an official décimo in a special edition: it has the same prize and the same odds as any other. What makes it special is the presentation, ideal for gifting. So in your message, promise hope and affection, not prizes.

Where can I get a superdécimo to give as a gift?

At an official lottery office. You can see the Christmas superdécimo edition and reserve it on our website. As it's a limited edition, units are limited, so if you want to give one, it's best not to leave it to the last minute.
