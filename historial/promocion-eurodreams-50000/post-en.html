What EuroDreams is and how to play it

 

EuroDreams is the European lottery of monthly income: instead of a single payment, the prize is collected in instalments every month for years. In Spain it is run by the Spanish State Lottery (Loterías y Apuestas del Estado, SELAE) and it is also played in other European countries. Its great appeal is precisely that: turning luck into steady income that lasts over time.

How to fill in a bet

Playing is very simple. Each bet consists of choosing 6 numbers from 1 to 40 and, in addition, one Dream number from 1 to 5. You can mark the figures yourself or let the system pick them at random. Each bet costs €2.50. The Dream number is the key to the first prize: you have to match the 6 numbers and the Dream to take home the promotion's €50,000-a-month income.

When the draws take place

EuroDreams holds two draws a week: on Mondays and Thursdays. That gives you two chances a week to go for the prize while the promotion lasts. As with any game of chance, each draw is independent and every combination has the same probability, so there is no number or strategy that guarantees a win: you play for the thrill of that monthly income.

If you want to cover more combinations, EuroDreams also allows multiple bets, marking more numbers or more than one Dream number, so that several combinations are generated and played at once. They cost more, naturally, because you pay for each combination, but they are an option if you fancy widening your chances in a particular draw. At the lottery office we work out the exact cost for you before validating, so there are no surprises.

Conditions: who can play

 

EuroDreams has very specific conditions for taking part, and one of them sets it apart from other games: you can't play in a group. It is worth being clear about them before you take part:

→ Individuals only: taking part is reserved for individuals, not companies or organisations.

→ Adults only: you must be of legal age in order to play.

→ Individual bets: each bet is placed individually, in the name of a single person.

→ No groups or syndicates: unlike other games, with EuroDreams it is not allowed to share bets or take part in syndicates.

That last condition is important and tends to catch people out: if you are used to playing the lottery in a syndicate with family or workmates, bear in mind that EuroDreams is a strictly individual game. Everyone plays their own bet, and only the person named on it can claim the prize.
