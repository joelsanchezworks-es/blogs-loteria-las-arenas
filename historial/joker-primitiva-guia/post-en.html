The quick answer

The Joker is an optional €1 game with a 7-digit number and prizes from €1 up to €1,000,000

When you play La Primitiva you mark the Joker box and, for an extra €1 per draw, the system assigns you a random 7-digit number. If you match digits of the winning number — consecutively and from the right — you win a fixed prize, ranging from €1 for the last digit up to €1,000,000 for all seven.

What is the Joker in La Primitiva?

 

The Joker is a complementary game on La Primitiva. It isn't a standalone draw you can play on its own: it's always attached to a La Primitiva bet (and it's also available with El Gordo de la Primitiva). Think of it as an optional 'add-on' that rides on your ticket to give you a second route to a prize.

Unlike the six numbers on La Primitiva, which you choose yourself, you don't pick the Joker number: when you validate the bet, the computer system at Loterías y Apuestas del Estado (the Spanish State Lottery) assigns you a random 7-digit code. That number is printed on your receipt and is the one in play. It's unique to every ticket.

The beauty of the Joker is twofold: on one hand, it's very cheap — €1 — and on the other, it pays out fixed prizes, that is, set amounts that don't depend on how many people play or on the sales revenue. Match a category and you collect exactly what the table says. No more, no less. And at the very top of that table, waiting, sits one million euros.

It's no last-minute gimmick: the Joker has been La Primitiva's star add-on for years, and that's precisely why it's worth knowing well. It's one of those extras people tick 'just in case' without ever fully understanding how it works, and that's exactly why so many small and medium prizes go unclaimed every year. Understanding it takes five minutes; ignoring it can cost you a prize.

How to play the Joker and how much it costs

 

Playing the Joker is as easy as marking a box, but it's worth being clear on a couple of details so there are no surprises with the price or with your entry:

→ Mark the 'Joker' box on your La Primitiva ticket (or ask your trusted lottery office to add it when they validate).

→ It costs €1 per draw, added to the price of your La Primitiva bet. If you play the Joker for Thursday and Saturday, it's €1 per draw.

→ You don't choose the number: when you validate, the system automatically assigns you a random 7-digit number.

→ Check it on the receipt: that 7-digit number is printed on your receipt. If it doesn't appear, the Joker hasn't been added.

→ Available with La Primitiva and El Gordo de la Primitiva, the draws in the Primitiva family (Thursday, Saturday and Sunday).

In short: an X, a euro and a seven-digit number you don't control but that could give you a very pleasant surprise. The only thing you have to do is make sure the number is printed on your receipt.

How to check the Joker (from right to left)

 

This is the part that's misread the most, so take your time with it. To find out whether your Joker has won, you compare the digits of your number with those of the winning number from the right and consecutively. Matching odd digits here and there, or starting from the left, doesn't count: you count how many digits in a row match, starting from the last one (the units digit).

It's crystal clear with an example. Imagine the winning Joker number is 3 5 8 1 2 4 9 and yours is 7 0 6 1 2 4 9:

ItemExample

Matching from the right4 consecutive digits (1-2-4-9)
Category and prize4th category → €1,000

The last four digits match (1-2-4-9), so the prize is €1,000. Even if the first three were different, it doesn't matter: only the run from the right counts.

See where the danger lies? If that same person had compared their number from left to right, they'd have seen that the first three digits (7-0-6 against 3-5-8) don't match and might have wrongly concluded they'd 'won nothing'. And yet they had €1,000 waiting. Reading the Joker from the wrong side, or not reading it at all, is exactly the mistake we turn to next.

Frequently asked questions

 
What exactly is the Joker in La Primitiva?

It's a complementary game added to your La Primitiva bet (and it's also available with El Gordo de la Primitiva). For an extra €1 per draw, the system assigns you a random 7-digit number. If those digits match the winning number — consecutively and from the right — you win a fixed prize, ranging from €1 up to €1,000,000. It isn't a draw you can play separately: it's always tied to La Primitiva.

How much does it cost to play the Joker, and can I choose the number?

It costs €1 per draw, added to the price of your La Primitiva bet. If you play the Joker for the Thursday draw and the Saturday one, you pay €1 for each. And no, you can't choose the number: it's generated randomly when you validate the bet and is printed on your receipt. How do you check whether the Joker has won a prize?

You compare the digits of your number with those of the winning number starting from the right (the last digit, the units digit) and counting how many match consecutively. Odd digits here and there or starting from the left don't count: only the run from the right matters.
