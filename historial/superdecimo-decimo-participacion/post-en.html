There's more than one way to play the Christmas Lottery, and they don't all cost the same or suit the same occasions. The décimo (a full lottery ticket) is the classic option; the participación (a share) lets you play for less; and the superdécimo is a special edition designed for gifting or for a number with a story.

The usual question is which one to choose. The answer comes down to three things: how much you want to spend, how you want to play (on your own or as a group) and whether it's for you or a gift. Let's compare them so you have it clear.

In brief

Décimo to play yourself, participación for a group, superdécimo to gift

All three are official lottery tickets and have the same probability of a prize. The décimo (20 €) gives you the number's full prize. The participación costs less and gives you the share proportional to what you play. The superdécimo is an official décimo in a special edition, ideal for gifting or collecting, with the same prize as a décimo.

The décimo: playing with the whole prize

 

The décimo is the classic way to play: you buy the whole number at its official price (20 €) and, if it wins, the prize for that décimo is entirely yours.

It's the best option if you want to play yourself, safe in the knowledge that you're not sharing the prize with anyone. A décimo can be given as a gift too, though for a gift with more charm there's the superdécimo.

The superdécimo: a special number to gift or collect

 

The Christmas superdécimo is an official décimo presented in a special edition. Inside it's official lottery, with the same workings and the same prize as any décimo; what changes is the presentation and its limited-edition character, designed to make it a memorable gift or a collector's piece.

It's ideal when you want to give something more special than a standalone décimo, or when you're after a number with a story. That said, like any décimo, it doesn't increase the odds of a prize: what it adds is the excitement and the presentation. You can see the edition and the price on the superdécimo page.

The participación: playing as a group or on a small budget

 

The participación is a share, a fraction of a décimo: it lets you play a number for an amount lower than the 20 € of a full décimo. In return, the prize you get is the proportional share of what you've played, not the whole prize for the number.

It's the typical choice for playing as a group (the office syndicate, the family, the group of friends) or when you want to take part on a small budget. If you play as a group, it's worth being clear about who's taking part and for how much, so the split is clean if it wins.

So, which one suits you?

 

There's no option that's better than another: there's one that fits you best. To decide, think about your own situation:

→ You want to play yourself and keep the whole prize: the décimo.

→ You want to give a special gift or a number with a story: the superdécimo.

→ You want to play as a group or on a small budget: the participación.

→ You want to combine several: you can, for example, play your own décimo and gift a superdécimo.

Whatever you decide, always do it at an official lottery office, at the official price and with your ticket checked and kept safe until the draw.

Frequently asked questions about the superdécimo, décimo and participación

 
What's the difference between a décimo and a superdécimo?

Inside, none when it comes to the game: the superdécimo is an official décimo, with the same workings and the same prize. The difference is in the presentation: the superdécimo is a special edition, designed for gifting or collecting, whereas the décimo is the classic format you buy to play at the official price of 20 €. You can see the superdécimo's edition and price on its page.

Does the superdécimo have a better chance of winning?

No. The superdécimo is an official décimo and, like every number, has exactly the same probability of a prize. There's no option —décimo, superdécimo or participación— that increases your chances of winning. What changes is the price, the share of the prize you get and, in the case of the superdécimo, the special presentation.

How much do I get if a participación wins?

The proportional share of what you've played. If a participación represents, say, a fraction of a décimo, you collect that same fraction of the number's prize, not the décimo's full prize. That's why a participación costs less than 20 €: you're playing only a part. If you play as a group, it's worth putting in writing who's taking part and for how much.

Which is best for a gift?

For a gift, the superdécimo is usually the option with the most charm: it's an official décimo in a special edition, designed precisely for giving or collecting. An ordinary décimo can be gifted too, and a participación works for a more affordable token or for including someone in a syndicate. It depends on your budget and the effect you're after.

Can I combine several formats?

Yes, no problem at all. Plenty of people play their own décimo and, on top of that, gift a superdécimo to a relative or join in with a participación in the work syndicate. They're not mutually exclusive: you can choose the format that fits each situation (your own play, your gifts, your groups).

Are all three formats official lottery?

Yes. The décimo, the superdécimo and the participación are official lottery from the State National Lottery, with every guarantee, as long as you buy them at an official lottery office. Buying from an authorised office assures you of the official price, an official product and payment of the prize with every guarantee.
