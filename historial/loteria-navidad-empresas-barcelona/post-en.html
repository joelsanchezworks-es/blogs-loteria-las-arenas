To organise a company's Christmas Lottery in Barcelona, it is worth choosing a format that suits the location and the team's working hours. In-person purchase may be practical for some people; others will need to buy online or consider a different physical delivery.

In brief

The format is chosen according to your site and your working hours

There is no single way to organise it: it depends on whether staff are at one site or several, on the shifts and on hybrid working. Being in Barcelona, close to the lottery office, does not in itself mean a visit, a free distribution or an immediate service: those conditions are agreed. The first thing is to define the campaign; then it is communicated to the team.

Does the whole team work at the same site?

 

If staff are all at one site, consider whether a group delivery would be worthwhile, or a campaign in which each worker handles their own purchase individually. The choice also depends on who can coordinate receipt and distribution.

If there are several shifts, do not assume that everyone will be able to buy or collect an internal delivery within the same time slot. It is best to identify that need before the arrangements are finalised, not once the décimos (lottery tickets) have already arrived.

In hybrid teams, avoid designing the campaign solely for those who are in the office every day. A single, shared communication can include the options actually agreed for the staff as a whole.

In-person purchase at Lotería Las Arenas

 

The campaign can include in-person purchase at our lottery office. Give each worker the relevant instructions for identifying their company's reservation, and advise them to check the current opening hours before travelling.

Being nearby does not change the format of a purchase already made: décimos bought with online deposit are held in safekeeping. They should not be advertised as available for later collection at the counter.

If in-person purchase is offered, also confirm its deadline. It need not coincide with the closing date for the same company's online purchases.

An online space for staff

 

We can set up a private virtual space with a password and instructions. The organiser shares the campaign access, and staff buy according to the formats enabled.

Deposit and safekeeping allow people to buy without receiving the décimos physically. If someone needs them on paper, they should use a planned delivery option, rather than choosing deposit in the belief that it will later turn into a collection.

Home delivery is another possible format. Its terms and cost must be checked for the order's destination, without assuming uniform rates for all locations.

Group delivery and sale on the premises: two different things

 

In a group delivery, the arrival at the company of the purchased décimos is coordinated, together with the list of buyers. The organisers must plan who receives the delivery and how it is distributed.

A reservation for direct sale on the premises follows a different approach. Before announcing it, its availability, arrangements and dates must be settled with Las Arenas.

It should not be promised that any Barcelona company gets an included visit, free distribution or an immediate service. Those conditions require an agreement, even if the lottery office is close to the workplace.

A calendar that takes your activity into account

 

Agree the closing date and, if there is a physical delivery, the expected receipt date. As an organising principle, bear in mind holidays, shifts and office closures, because they can affect your ability to distribute the décimos.

One company may need to close its campaign earlier than another. The general date of the draw does not replace the purchase and delivery conditions that have been agreed.

Nor is it a good idea to copy the previous year's poster without reviewing the number, the access and the deadlines. The poster and instructions for the current campaign should be the reference the team receives.

What to send us to prepare a proposal

 

With a few details we can check availability and look at a suitable set-up, without committing to a format before it is agreed. This is what helps us prepare your proposal:

→ The company name and the participating location or locations.

→ The approximate number of décimos.

→ Whether each worker will buy on their own or the company will place an order to give as gifts.

→ Your number preference.

→ Whether you need a physical delivery (and of what kind).
