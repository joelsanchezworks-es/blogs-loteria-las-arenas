When to buy to be in time

 

The Christmas Lottery goes on sale in summer, so you have months ahead of you… but the most sought-after numbers fly off the shelves. You can buy right up to 22 December itself, although leaving it to the last minute carries a risk: that your number is no longer available.

If you want to know exactly when it goes on sale and how to secure your number, we explain it in detail in when you can buy the Christmas Lottery 2026. The simple rule: the sooner, the better.

The day of the draw: 22 December

 

The Christmas Special Draw takes place on the morning of 22 December 2026, live from Madrid. That day all the prizes are called out: the Gordo, the 2nd, the 3rd, the fourths, the fifths, the pedrea (small prizes)… and the reintegro (refund), which gives you back the €20 for the décimo if the last digit matches.

If you want to know what time it starts and how long it lasts, you'll find it in the Christmas Lottery timetable and in what time the draw starts and ends. And remember: all numbers have the same probability.

After the draw: the claim period

 

If your décimo wins a prize, there's no rush… but no infinite time either: you have 3 months to claim it. The deadline counts from the day after the draw, so for the draw on 22 December 2026 you can collect until the end of March 2027.

Where you collect depends on the amount: prizes of up to €2,000 are paid at lottery offices and points of sale; larger ones, at authorised banks, with your ID document. And watch out for tax: the first €40,000 are exempt and 20 % is taxed on the rest.

And in January, the El Niño draw

 

The festive calendar doesn't end in December: on 6 January 2027 comes the El Niño Special Draw. It's the second big date, with which many 'rekindle' the excitement after Christmas.

It has its own dates, prices and prizes, which you can check in the El Niño Lottery 2027: prices and key dates. If you play both, the calendar makes it easy: one after the other.

Frequently asked questions about the Christmas Lottery 2026 calendar

 
What day is the Christmas Lottery 2026 draw?

The Christmas Special Draw takes place on 22 December 2026, in the morning and live from Madrid. It's the central date of the calendar, although the sale starts months earlier and the claim period extends after the draw.

Until when can I buy the Christmas Lottery?

You can buy right up to the day of the draw, 22 December. The sale starts in summer, so you have many months; but the most sought-after numbers sell out sooner, so it's wise to buy in advance. We detail it in our article on when you can buy the Christmas Lottery 2026.

How long do I have to claim a prize?

3 months from the day after the draw. For the draw on 22 December 2026, the deadline runs until the end of March 2027. Prizes of up to €2,000 are collected at lottery offices; larger ones, at authorised banks, with your ID document.

When is the El Niño draw 2027?

On 6 January 2027. It's the other big date of the festive season, with its own dates, prices and prizes. You can check the detail in our article on the El Niño Lottery 2027.

What is the reintegro (refund) in the Christmas Lottery?

The reintegro (refund) is the last digit of the number that wins the Gordo. If your décimo ends in that digit, you get back the €20 it cost you, even if you have no other prize. It's the smallest prize, but also the most frequent.
