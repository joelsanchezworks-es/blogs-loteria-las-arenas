How to present it

 

The beauty of the superdécimo is that it already comes with a special presentation, so half the gift is done for you. Even so, you can add your own touch: slip it inside a card, pair it with a little something or save the moment to give it in person.

Don't take away its charm by rushing. A superdécimo handed over calmly, looking each other in the eye and with a “this is so we can celebrate it together” is worth far more than leaving it on a table.

What message to add

 

One line is enough to make the gift even better. The tone depends on the person:

→ Romantic (partner): “May luck be with us this year… and the years to come”.

→ Warm (family): “In case we get to celebrate it all together”.

→ Fun (friends): “If it wins, you know who shared it with you”.

→ Team (work): “May the excitement (and the prize) be for everyone”.

Nothing elaborate is needed: a sincere message, with the person's name, turns a superdécimo into a keepsake.

When to give it

 

The sooner you give it, the longer the excitement lasts. If you hand it over at the office lunch, at a dinner with friends or at a December family gathering, whoever receives it will enjoy the wait until the draw.

What's more, giving it in good time has a practical advantage: the superdécimo is a limited edition, so getting hold of it early ensures you have it for when you want to give it. Don't leave it until the last day.
