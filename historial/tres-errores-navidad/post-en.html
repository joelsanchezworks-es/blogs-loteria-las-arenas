Every Christmas we see the same three mistakes repeat: buying your décimo (a tenth-share of a ticket) early and losing it, not checking it and letting it expire, and sharing it 'by word of mouth'. The first two are maddening; the third can get very expensive.

The good news is that all three can be avoided with one simple step. Here they are, in order, each with its fix.

In brief

The 3 mistakes (and how to fix them)

1) Buying your décimo early and losing it → leave it in secure deposit (safekeeping), kept safe. 2) Not checking it and letting it expire after 90 days → check it in time. 3) Sharing it by word of mouth → put it in writing, who puts in how much. The third is the one that causes the most grief.

Mistake 1: buying your décimo… and losing it

 

It's the classic one: you buy your décimo in November, full of excitement, and in December it's nowhere to be found. In a drawer, in a coat, in a bag you no longer use. And with no décimo, there's no prize: it's a bearer instrument (al portador).

The solution we give at the lottery office is simple: leave it in secure deposit (safekeeping). If you buy it with us, it can stay in our safe until the draw, in your name. Zero risk of misplacing it.

Mistake 2: not checking it… and letting it expire after 90 days

 

It sounds unbelievable, but it happens: winning décimos that nobody ever claims because they aren't checked in time. Lottery prizes have an expiry deadline of 3 months (90 days), counted from the day after the draw. Once that deadline passes, the prize is lost.

The remedy costs nothing: check your décimo after the draw, even if you're sure it didn't win. A reintegro (stake refund) or the pedrea (small prizes) count too, and they expire too.

Mistake 3 (the most expensive): sharing a décimo by word of mouth

 

This is the one that truly hurts. A décimo shared 'by word of mouth' —among family, friends or colleagues— and, if it wins, the problems begin: who put in, who didn't, how big each person's share was. We've seen it end badly more times than we'd like; there are families who have ended up in court over a split that nobody ever made clear.

The solution is as easy as it is awkward to ask for in the moment: put it in writing. A list of who's taking part and with how much, before the draw, and keep a copy of the décimo. If you like, we explain it in more detail in sharing lottery on WhatsApp and its legal risks. This is general information, not legal advice.

The golden rule: put it in writing and keep it safe

If you take away just one idea, let it be this: the décimo, kept safe; the prize, checked in time; and anything shared, in writing. Three one-minute steps that spare you the three most typical headaches of Christmas.

Frequently asked questions about the most common mistakes with your Christmas décimo

 
What is secure deposit (safekeeping) for a décimo?

It means leaving your décimo kept at the lottery office, in your name, until the draw. If you buy it with us, it can stay in our safe: that way there's no risk of losing it or misplacing it between November and December.

How long do you have to claim a lottery prize?

3 months (90 days), counted from the day after the draw. Once that deadline passes, the prize expires and is lost. That's why it's worth checking your décimo even if you're sure it didn't win.

Why is it risky to share a décimo by word of mouth?

Because the décimo is a bearer instrument (al portador) and, if it isn't clear who's taking part and with how much, a prize can end up in an argument (or something worse). Putting it in writing before the draw heads off almost any problem.

How do I put a shared décimo in writing?

With a simple list: who's in, with what share, plus a copy or photo of the décimo, signed or shared with everyone before the draw. Nothing formal is needed; it's enough that it's on record. For large amounts, it's best to get advice.

Do a reintegro (stake refund) or the pedrea (small prizes) expire too?

Yes. Any prize —the reintegro or the pedrea included— has the same 3-month deadline. However small it is, if you don't claim it in time, it's lost. Checking takes a minute.
