The most common doubt when buying the Christmas Lottery online is not whether it works, but what you receive in exchange for your money. It makes sense: you don't walk away with a piece of paper in your hand, so you want to know that there's a record of your purchase.

The short answer is that, when you buy on the official website of a lottery office, paying online is safe and leaves you with real proof of purchase, not a promise. Here are the questions we're asked most, answered one by one.

In brief

When you buy the Christmas Lottery online you pay securely (usually by card), you receive an instant confirmation of the purchase and your décimo is registered in your name. Can you play the Christmas Lottery online?

 

Yes, and with the same guarantees as over the counter. You can buy your Christmas Lottery online on the official website of a lottery office: the décimo is official, the price is the official one (€20) and the purchase is registered in your name.

The difference isn't in the security, but in the convenience: you buy from home, at any time, and you don't depend on a piece of paper that you could lose.

Which payment methods are accepted?

 

The usual way to play online is to pay by bank card (debit or credit). Some lottery offices also accept other methods; you'll see them available at the moment of payment, in the secure payment gateway of the official website.

Whatever the method, you pay the official price of the décimo, with no surcharges for buying online. If a website charges you more or asks you for strange payment methods, be wary: always buy from an official lottery office.

When does the purchase confirmation arrive?

 

Instantly. As soon as the payment is completed, you receive a confirmation of the purchase (in your account and, usually, also by email), with your décimo registered in your name.

That confirmation is your guarantee that the transaction has gone through correctly. If you pay and no confirmation arrives, don't assume the purchase is done: check your account or contact the lottery office before trying again.

How do I save my proof of purchase?

 

When you buy online there's no paper ticket: your proof of purchase is digital, and that's its advantage. It's stored in your account, in your name, and you can check it whenever you like. You can't lose it or forget where you left it.

What happens after you pay?

 

Your décimo is registered in your name and you choose how you want it. You can leave it in safekeeping at the lottery office (they store it and check it for you) or ask them to send it to your home if you prefer to have it physically.

If your décimo wins a prize, the payout is handled according to the terms of your purchase: with safekeeping, the lottery office checks it and notifies you. In any case, you don't depend on a piece of paper that you could lose.

More frequently asked questions about paying for the Christmas Lottery online

 
Is it safe to pay for the Christmas Lottery online?

Yes, as long as you do it on the official website of a certified lottery office. The payment is made in a secure payment gateway, the décimo is official, the price is the official one (€20) and the purchase is registered in your name. The risk isn't 'paying online' in itself, but doing it on unauthorised websites or ones with a mark-up.

What can I pay with?

The usual way is to pay by bank card (debit or credit). Some lottery offices accept additional methods, which you'll see at the moment of payment. In all cases you pay the official price of the décimo, with no surcharges for buying it online.

Does paying online cost more than at the lottery office?

No. The price is the official one (€20 for the décimo), whether you buy it online or over the counter, with no commissions or surcharges for doing it online. If a website charges you more, it's a warning sign.

When do I know my purchase has been confirmed?

Instantly. As soon as the payment is completed you receive a confirmation in your account and, usually, by email. If it doesn't arrive, don't assume the purchase is done: check your account or contact the lottery office before paying again.

Where is my proof of purchase if I don't have paper?

Your proof of purchase is digital and is stored in your account, in your name. You can check it whenever you like. It replaces the paper ticket, so there's no ticket to lose. Even so, you can also save the confirmation email.

In whose name is a décimo bought online registered?

In your name: the purchase is associated with your verified account. That's one of the advantages of playing online compared with paper, where the décimo is 'to the bearer' and depends on who has it physically.

Can I choose to have the décimo sent to my home?

Yes. After paying, you can request home delivery if you prefer to have the décimo physically, or leave it in safekeeping at the lottery office, where it's stored and checked. Both options are valid; you choose according to what's most convenient for you.

If I win having paid online, how do I claim?

The payout is handled according to the terms of your purchase. If you left the décimo in safekeeping, the lottery office checks it and notifies you if there's a prize. As the purchase is in your name, you don't depend on keeping a piece of paper. For large prizes, the usual payout procedure with identification is followed.
